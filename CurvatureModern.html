Two independently rendered Physical scenes · same observer, towers, fixed Eye-level camera and field of view in each viewport. Surface geometry, atmosphere and engineered profile parameters are set independently for A and B. Compare deliberately ignores curvature-dependent Scene horizon aiming. Resizing from Scene to two narrower viewports changes pixels per degree, not the angular field of view. The orange horizon marker, when resolved, is derived from forward-traced first-ground-contact rays and is not a guarantee of unique global visibility.
